Epic Audio Games

Talk your way through audio adventures. Listen, answer out loud, and play!

Stories and games you play with your voice. Put on your headphones, listen, and answer out loud, type it, or tap one of the quick replies. Every word is shown on screen too, so you can read along.

  • Plays offline
  • No account
  • No ads
  • Eight games

How it plays

Nothing to learn. The game talks, and you talk back.

1

Put on your headphones

Pick a game and press play. Each one is a story told to you, scene by scene, with characters, music and sound effects.

2

Listen

The characters ask you what to do. Every word they say appears on screen as they say it, so you never miss a line.

3

Answer out loud

Say your answer, type it, or tap a quick reply. The story goes where you take it.
