Epic Audio Games is eight voice-first audio games for iPhone and iPad, designed for blind and partially sighted players first. No account, login or demo account is needed. Every game plays offline; only packs need a connection.

HOW TO PLAY
- The first launch plays a short intro (tap to skip), then a welcome you can skip. The tabs: Games, Shop, Help, Settings.
- Tap a game on the Games tab. It talks, and every line appears in the chat as it is spoken.
- When it's your turn, a short sound plays and the mic opens by itself (with VoiceOver on, it waits: tap the big circle or the mic button, or use Magic Tap). Answer by speaking, typing, or tapping one of the options at the end of the chat. Tapping is the quickest way to test.
- Tap the big circle to skip the voice. The game's menu (three dots) has "Start again", "More stories and levels" and "How to play"; the privacy policy and support page are in Settings.
- If nobody answers, the question plays again; a second silence pauses the game until you tap.
- Noodle Rush is the shortest game: a few minutes to an ending.
- iPad: any orientation and window size; a wide window puts a game's controls beside its chat. Keys: Space is the big circle, Escape pauses, Command-1 to 4 pick a tab.

PERMISSIONS
The welcome explains the microphone before iOS asks for it and for speech recognition ("Not now" skips). Both serve only to hear answers while a game is open (on the device where it can, else by Apple's speech service); the app doesn't store or send recordings. Without them, typing and tapping work.

BACKGROUND AUDIO
Players often pocket a locked phone. While a game is open, the voice plays and the game listens with the screen locked (background audio mode), so the microphone indicator stays on until the player leaves the game. The lock screen and headphone buttons pause, carry on, skip or answer.

APPLE WATCH
The watch app is a remote for the game on the iPhone, with no content of its own: with a game open there, it shows the game's state, one big button (skip, talk, stop listening or carry on) and Pause, and buzzes as the mic opens.

IN-APP PURCHASES
Three non-consumable packs:
- frootopia_stories: The Kingdom of Frootopia, stories 2 to 5 (15 MB)
- alien_customs_levels: Alien Customs, levels 6 to 15 (26 MB)
- the_werewolf_stories: The Werewolf, stories 6 to 50 (133 MB)

To reach them: the Shop tab (each pack's "Buy for" price, and "Restore purchases"); the "Get" button on a game's card (such as "Get 45 more mysteries" on The Werewolf); the end of Frootopia's story 1; or a game's menu, "More stories and levels".

A bought pack downloads over HTTPS from packs.epicaudiogames.com, is checked against the app's built-in checksum, and installs; the game carries on at "Next chapter".

USAGE DATA
The app sends usage data to our own server under a random ID made on the device: the tabs and games opened, how far games get, and purchase and download results. Never audio, what the player says or types, or anything about VoiceOver or accessibility settings. No third-party SDKs, and nothing is shared with other companies or used for ads or tracking (so no tracking prompt). It's on by default, stated on the welcome page with a "Turn off" button; Settings > Privacy turns it off and deletes it. Details: https://epicaudiogames.com/privacy#usage-data

CONTENT
Nuclear War is a light-hearted strategy game for one player. The player picks any of five countries (France, the USA, the UK, China or Russia); the game runs the other four, and any country can be a target, so no real country or government is singled out. The leaders have fictional parody names. Each round they spend money on shields, sanctions and bombs, and the announcer says when a city has been destroyed: all spoken, with no images of violence and nothing graphic. Pirate Quest's dice game is played for in-game coins only. The app has no ads, web views, chat or user accounts.
