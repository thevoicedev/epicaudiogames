## Title

Epic Audio Games

## Short description (80 characters at most)

Talk your way through audio adventures. Listen, answer out loud, and play!

## Full description

Epic Audio Games are stories and games you play with your voice. Put on your headphones, listen, and answer out
loud, type your answer, or tap a button. Every word is shown on screen too, so you can read along.

Everything plays offline, with no account and no ads.

**The games:**

- **The Kingdom of Frootopia:** join Cosmo and friends on a galaxy adventure. Story 1 is free; four more stories
  come in a pack.
- **Noodle Rush:** you're broke and starving. Talk your way to a free bowl of ramen, and find all ten endings.
- **Signal Decoders:** help Alex and Robin decode a mysterious alien signal in a blacked-out London. Five chapters.
- **Pirate Quest:** roll the dice, pick your crew and conquer the seas.
- **Leaning Tower of Pizza:** tell lies to grow Pinocchio's nose and save the town from the evil pizza bot.
- **Alien Customs:** convince a strict alien customs officer to let you into Jupiter with your suspicious luggage.
  5 levels free, more in a pack.
- **The Werewolf:** find out which villager is the werewolf. 5 stories free, 45 more in packs.
- **Nuclear War:** lead your country to victory in a nuclear battle.

New stories and levels come in packs you can unlock.

## To decide before publishing

- **The game list.** The first test build has Frootopia, Noodle Rush and Signal Decoders. The other five games come
  in phases 3 and 5. List only the games the published version has.
- **Names.** "Signal Decoders" is called "Alien Invasion: Signal Decoders" on its cover art. "The Kingdom of
  Frootopia" is also shortened to "King of Frootopia".
- **Age rating and audience.** Nuclear War's theme decides the content rating. If children are the audience,
  Google Play's Families policy applies: a privacy policy covering microphone use, and no ads.
- **Speech.** Speech recognition runs on the device where it can. Some phones need their offline English speech
  pack downloaded once, and some use Google's online service when they don't have one.
