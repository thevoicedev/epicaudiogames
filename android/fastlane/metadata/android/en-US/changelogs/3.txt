A new design, made for blind and partially sighted players first: clearer text in three sizes, dark, light and high-contrast themes, four tabs (Games, Shop, Help, Settings), help pages you can listen to, voice speed, music volume, more time to answer, and layouts for tablets. A sound plays when the microphone opens, and with a screen reader on it no longer opens by itself. The app now shares usage data with us under a random ID (never what you say or type). You can turn it off in Settings.
