Epic Audio Games are stories and games you play with your voice. Put on your headphones, listen, and answer out loud. The characters ask you what to do, and the story goes where you take it.

It works like a chat. Every word appears on screen as it's spoken, so you can read along. When a question comes, the mic opens by itself. Say your answer, type it, or tap one of the options at the end of the chat. You can answer while the voice is still talking.

EIGHT GAMES

The Kingdom of Frootopia
Join Cosmo and friends on a galaxy adventure, and save the Kingdom of Frootopia. Story 1 is free. The Stories 2 to 5 pack has the rest of the adventure.

Noodle Rush
You're broke and starving. Talk your way to a free bowl of ramen. There are 10 endings to find.

Signal Decoders
Help Alex and Robin decode a mysterious alien signal in a blacked-out London. All five chapters are free.

Pirate Quest
Gather your crew, roll the dice and outwit rival captains on the hunt for the English fortune. The whole quest is free.

Leaning Tower of Pizza
Tell lies to grow Pinocchio's nose, and save Venice from the evil pizza robot. Bananas are berries: true or false? Battle and challenge modes, both free.

Alien Customs
Convince a strict alien customs officer to let you into Jupiter with your suspicious luggage. Levels 1 to 5 are free. The 10 More Levels pack adds levels 6 to 15.

The Werewolf
Listen to the villagers and find out which of them is the werewolf, before it strikes again. Stories 1 to 5 are free. The 45 Mysteries pack adds stories 6 to 50.

Nuclear War
Play as France, the USA, the UK, China or Russia through five rounds of nuclear war. Shield your cities, sanction your rivals and choose when to strike. The whole war is free.

PACKS

Three games have a pack with more stories or levels. Each pack is a one-time purchase, with no subscription. A pack downloads once, then plays offline like the rest.

GOOD TO KNOW

- No ads, no account, and no tracking.
- The games are inside the app, so they play offline. Buying and downloading a pack needs a connection.
- Your phone's speech recognition (usually Google's) turns what you say into words. Many phones do this on the phone itself. Others use an online speech service, which needs a connection. Typing and tapping always work.
- Your place in each game is saved at every question, so you can pick up where you left off.
